## 2. Why this is not "another app that looks like the others"
- The product is **a managed marketplace with money in the middle** (vetting + KYC + escrow + dispute resolution + shipping), not a social feed, link directory or template. Similar apps are mostly open freelancer boards or creator lists with off-platform payments.
- Everything operational lives in one account: wallet, invoices, shipments, deliverables, revisions, payouts. A creator or brand cannot complete the core job in a browser tab as conveniently: shipping updates, review deadlines and payout alerts arrive as native push notifications, and uploads use the phone camera and photo library.
- The UI is app-specific: 41 phone screens (`ugcad-frontend/src/app/screens`: brand 18, creator 13, shared 10) with bottom tabs, bottom sheets, safe areas and back gestures; it is not the desktop site.

## 5. Do NOT claim (not verified, or not true)
- "AI matching" or "AI anything": not implemented.
- "Subscription plans / Pro / Growth / Scale": UI exists but purchase is "Coming soon"; hidden in the shell.
- "Expert editing add-on": "Coming soon".
- "Instant payouts": payouts follow the payout schedule and admin approval (`/api/admin/withdrawals/{id}/approve`).
- "Aadhaar eKYC / DigiLocker": Aadhaar is an optional typed number with a checksum, reviewed by staff.
- "Fully offline" or "works without internet": the app loads the live service.
- "Verified" for every creator: only those whose KYC is approved carry the badge.
- Twitter/X and YouTube pages: marked coming soon.
- Anything about location, nearby creators, or social-media feed integrations.
